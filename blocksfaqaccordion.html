MMS-4 is a three-day immersive event centered on meditation, consciousness, and community, inspired by the teachings of Brahmarshi Subhash Patriji. It brings together spiritual leaders, meditation guides, and seekers for live music meditations, talks, cultural experiences, and mindful connections.

Open to everyone regardless of background, it's designed to help attendees learn, grow, and deepen their spiritual practice as part of a global meditation community.

Attending gives you access to group and music meditation, meaningful connections, and wisdom from global meditation masters that's hard to find elsewhere. It's an invitation to awaken, heal, and transform through a fully immersive experience.

Dates:
Friday, Sept 25 — 4:30 PM to 10:00 PM
Saturday, Sept 26 — 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Sunday, Sept 27 — 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Venue: Denmark High School, 645 Mullinax Rd, Alpharetta, GA 30004

Sessions cover breath-based meditation and pyramid science, mindfulness and stress relief, conscious/plant-based living and holistic health, plus spiritual growth and self-realization — designed to help attendees expand their consciousness.

It's free for kids under 13. Everyone else pays a nominal $25 fee, which covers high-vibe breakfast, lunch, and dinner throughout the event.

There's no minimum age to attend. Families bringing babies or toddlers are asked to be mindful during meditation sessions to keep the space peaceful for everyone.
